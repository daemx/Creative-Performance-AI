Creative Analysis

The business shows weak hook structures in short-form content. Product presentation appears too late in the videos, reducing retention and lowering click-through rates. Current creatives lack emotional tension, urgency, and clear value communication. UGC implementation appears limited and there is minimal storytelling in paid creatives.

Media Buyer Analysis

The advertising structure likely suffers from weak creative testing systems and insufficient segmentation between cold, warm, and hot audiences. The business may also be experiencing creative fatigue, inefficient retargeting structure, and weak offer positioning. Recommended improvements include:

• Faster hooks within first 2 seconds
• Aggressive creative testing
• UGC implementation
• Stronger CTA structure
• Landing page optimization
• Retargeting sequences
• Dynamic offer positioning

Social Media Analysis

Brand consistency appears moderate. Current content lacks premium positioning and authority-building assets. Posting structure should focus more on:

• Product demonstrations
• Problem/solution videos
• Founder storytelling
• Customer proof
• Educational hooks
